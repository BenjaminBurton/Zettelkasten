## ☸️ Kubernetes – Orchestrating the Cloud

Kubernetes is where Linux and automation meet scale.  
It’s the tool that turns individual containers into full systems that can self-heal, auto-scale, and survive anything.

This folder is where I document my hands-on work with Kubernetes — from running local clusters to designing production-grade architectures.

---

## 🔧 What You’ll Find Here

- **Core Concepts & YAML Deep Dives**  
  Pods, Deployments, Services, Ingress, ConfigMaps, and Secrets — explained, tested, and deployed by hand.

- **Cluster Setup & Management**  
  Building and managing clusters with `kind`, `k3d`, and `Minikube` locally.  
  Exploring `kubectl`, Helm, and the Kubernetes API directly.

- **Networking & Security**  
  Understanding how Kubernetes handles DNS, load balancing, and service discovery.  
  Setting up RBAC, namespaces, and network policies.

- **CI/CD & Automation**  
  Integrating GitLab CI, ArgoCD, and Python scripts to automate deployments.  
  Testing resilience and autoscaling under real traffic.

- **Monitoring & Observability**  
  Deploying Prometheus, Grafana, and Loki to track cluster health and performance.

---

## ⚙️ Projects & Experiments

- Deploying a full **2-tier web app** with high availability  
- Automating **namespace creation and policy enforcement**  
- Building a **local DevOps pipeline** to deploy containers from my laptop to Kubernetes
