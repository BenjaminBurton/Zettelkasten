## 🐧 Linux – The Foundation of Everything I Build

See also: [[Python]] | [[Kubernetes]]

Linux isn’t just an operating system — it’s the foundation of my entire journey.  
Everything I do in DevOps, automation, and cloud architecture starts here.  

This folder documents my exploration of Linux from the ground up — not just commands, but the principles and patterns that make it so powerful.  

---

## 🧠 What You’ll Find Here

- **System Internals & Architecture**  
  Breaking down how Linux boots, manages memory, handles processes, and keeps the system alive.  
  I study what happens from kernel to userland — because understanding that gives real power.

- **Filesystems & Storage**  
  Deep dives into how Linux handles files, permissions, and devices.  
  Experimenting with BTRFS, LVM, and encryption to understand performance and reliability.

- **Networking**  
  Exploring how packets move, how routing tables work, and how to configure everything from static IPs to iptables and nftables.

- **Shell Mastery**  
  Bash, Zsh, Tmux, Vim, and the dotfiles that power my workflow.  
  My goal is to be faster in the terminal than most are in a GUI.

- **System Administration & Automation**  
  Scripts, cron jobs, systemd services, and automation tools that make Linux maintenance effortless.
