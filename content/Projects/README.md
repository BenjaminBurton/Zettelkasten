## 🚀 Projects – Where Theory Meets Practice

Welcome to the **Projects** section of CLINative!  
This is where all my experiments, builds, and hands-on work live.  
Everything documented here is either:

- **A learning exercise** — trying out a new tool, command, or concept  
- **A practical project** — solving a real-world problem, automating workflows, or managing infrastructure  
- **A proof of concept** — testing ideas before integrating them into bigger systems

## 🌐 How I Use This Folder

Each project should have its own subfolder with a `README.md` explaining:

- The **goal** of the project  
- **How it works** — steps, architecture, or scripts  
- **Lessons learned** — what worked, what failed, and why  

Projects are connected to the rest of CLINative via **tags** and **wiki links** to maximize graph visibility and make exploration intuitive.
